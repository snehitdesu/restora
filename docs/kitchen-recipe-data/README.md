# Coders' Cafe kitchen data collection (for the kitchen to fill in)

RESTORA holds **61 draft recipes** for Coders' Cafe. Every quantity, method,
time and allergen in them was **estimated by RESTORA from the dish name**. None
of it comes from the kitchen, and none of it is verified. These sheets collect
the real figures. They are generated by
`npx tsx scripts/coders-cafe/kitchen-template.ts` from
`prisma/coders-cafe/recipes.ts`; regenerate them instead of editing the header
rows.

| Sheet | One row per | What the kitchen fills in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `recipe-lines.csv` | ingredient line of a draft recipe (429) | The real quantity and unit for **one base-size portion**, Y/N on whether the line belongs, any missing ingredient, the portion yield, and who checked it. |
| `ingredients.csv` | ingredient (54) | The allergens **with evidence** (label photo or supplier sheet), brand / supplier, pack size and the price actually paid. |
| `size-factors.csv` | larger size of a dish (24) | How much more a Medium / Large uses than the base size. Today RESTORA counts every size as 1× the base recipe. |
| `not-covered.csv` | dish with no draft (3 combos) | What the combo actually contains. |
| `recipe-signoff.csv` | draft recipe (61), in menu order | Nothing new: a tracker. Tick each step as it is done (lines weighed, size factors, allergen evidence, entered + *Confirm quantities*, approved by whom and when). A recipe is finished only when its last two columns are filled. |

Suggested order: work one category at a time from `recipe-signoff.csv`; for
each dish weigh the lines in `recipe-lines.csv`, then its sizes in
`size-factors.csv`; collect allergen evidence and paid prices per ingredient
in `ingredients.csv` once (ingredients are shared across dishes).

Rules for whoever fills these in:

1. **Weigh or measure, don't guess.** A "CONFIRMED" column is a statement by
   the person named in "Checked by" that this is what the kitchen uses.
2. **Allergens need evidence.** Leave "CONFIRMED allergens" empty when there is
   no label or supplier sheet; RESTORA keeps showing *NOT VERIFIED* until then.
   Never write "none" without evidence.
3. **Prices are what you paid**, from a bill, with its date, and say whether GST
   is included.
4. The draft columns are there only to speed you up. They are estimates.

How the answers get into RESTORA (by the owner or manager, not automatically):

- Quantities: **Recipes → the dish → Edit draft / lines**, then **Confirm
  quantities**, then **Approve**. Only an approved version drives stock
  consumption and food cost. RESTORA refuses to approve while the version is
  still marked *estimated*.
- Ingredient costs: record purchases (**Inventory → goods receipt**). Until a
  cost exists, the recipe shows *Cost unavailable*, never ₹0.00.
- Size factors: **Menu → the dish → its variant → consumption factor**.
- The 3 combos and the 10 unreadable menu-board entries
  (`prisma/coders-cafe/menu.ts`, `UNRESOLVED`) stay out of RESTORA until the
  owner provides their real contents, names and prices.
